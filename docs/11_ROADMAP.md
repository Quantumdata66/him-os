# Purpose

This document outlines the long-term strategic evolution of HIM OS.

It defines the phased release plan from initial single-user foundational release through executive UX/UI overhaul, integrations, AI orchestration, predictive intelligence, and multi-tenant SaaS platform capabilities.

# 📦 Version 1.0 — Foundational Release (Completed)

## Core Capabilities
- Single-user Personal Operating System foundation.
- Authentication, User Profile & Preferences.
- Basic Planning (Goals, Milestones, Daily Tasks).
- Knowledge Management & Notes Vault.
- Basic Financial Tracking & Account Balance Registry.
- Initial Project Management & Task Tracking.

---

# 🎨 Version 1.5 — Executive UX/UI Redesign (Active Focus)

## Core Objectives
- Complete UX redesign removing prototype legacy UI.
- Full Deep Forest Green (`#071A12`) Design System implementation.
- Restructuring around the 7 Canonical Life Destinations:
  1. `HOME` (`/dashboard`) — Executive Command Center & Morning Brief
  2. `TODAY` (`/today`) — Focused Execution, Top 3 MITs & Focus Session
  3. `BUILD` (`/build`) — Projects, Career Engine & Portfolio Showcase
  4. `LEARN` (`/learn`) — Books, Technical Courses & Goethe B1 German Anki Deck
  5. `GROW` (`/grow`) — Financial OS, Venture P&L, Currency Hedging & Tax Auditor
  6. `THINK` (`/think`) — Workspace Studio, Knowledge Graph & Decision Vault
  7. `REVIEW` (`/review`) — Daily/Weekly/Monthly Reviews & Automated Sprint Rollover
- Global Spotlight Search (`Ctrl + K`) & Universal Quick Capture (`+`).
- 100% clean compilation on Next.js 15 App Router across all 47 routes.

---

# ⚡ Version 2.0 — Automation & Ecosystem Integrations

## Planned Capabilities
- **OAuth Integrations**: Google Workspace (Calendar, Gmail, Drive), GitHub Repositories & Activity, Outlook, Notion importer.
- **Background Synchronization**: Celery workers & Redis message broker for background automation.
- **Smart Notifications**: Context-aware non-intrusive alerts for goal milestones, review reminders, and budget thresholds.
- **Offline Sync & PWA**: IndexedDB storage fallback with background resolution when reconnected.

---

# 🧠 Version 3.0 — AI Operating System & RAG

## Planned Capabilities
- **Executive AI Coach**: Multi-agent orchestration via LangGraph.
- **Specialized Sub-Agents**: Planning Agent, Learning Agent, Finance Agent, Career Agent, Research Agent, Review Agent.
- **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)**: Vector embeddings (`pgvector`) across markdown notes, research papers, book highlights, and review records.
- **Knowledge Graph Visualizer**: Contextual links between goals, skills, books, projects, and decisions.

---

# 🔮 Version 4.0 — Predictive Intelligence & Forecasting

## Planned Capabilities
- **Burnout Forecasting**: Workload & energy trend analysis.
- **Financial Projections**: Cash flow forecasting, tax liability estimation, and currency volatility hedging models.
- **Habit & Goal Completion Machine Learning**: Predictive analytics identifying probability of goal achievement based on historical consistency.

---

# 🌐 Version 5.0 — Multi-Tenant SaaS Platform

## Planned Capabilities
- **Enterprise Multi-Tenancy**: Organizations, teams, role-based access control (RBAC), and team workspaces.
- **Developer API & Marketplace**: Public REST/GraphQL APIs, webhooks, and third-party plugin marketplace.
- **SaaS Billing & Subscriptions**: Stripe billing integration, tier management, and enterprise deployment options.

---

# Final Principle

Roadmaps provide direction, not rigidity. Every release phase exists to serve one goal: transforming HIM OS into the definitive, trusted executive operating system for ambitious individuals.
