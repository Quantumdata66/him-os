# 01 — Product Vision

> **HPS Chapter Reference:** Chapter 2 & 3 — Mission, Vision & Product Philosophy

---

## 🚀 Mission Statement
To provide ambitious individuals with a unified operating system that transforms scattered intentions into disciplined execution.

## 💎 Product Philosophy
- **Execution Over Organization**: Organization exists only to enable execution.
- **Simplicity Creates Speed**: Powerful capabilities exist beneath simple interfaces.
- **Systems Beat Motivation**: Routine & structure outperform emotional momentum.
- **Progress Must Be Visible**: Tangible, observable growth keeps users focused.
