## 🧠 1. Core UX Principles

Every design decision throughout HIM OS must satisfy six guiding principles:

1. **Clarity Over Density**: The interface never displays every statistic simultaneously. Information is revealed progressively.
2. **Speed Feels Premium**: Interactions feel immediate. Page navigation completes in `< 100ms`, search in `< 200ms`.
3. **Focus Before Features**: Every screen answers one primary question (*"What is the next most important action?"*).
4. **Predictability**: Interactions behave consistently throughout the application.
5. **Progressive Complexity**: Beginners immediately understand the application; power users discover deeper capabilities naturally over time.
6. **Reward Progress**: Visible progress rings, streaks, and achievement badges celebrate consistency without distracting gamification.

## ⚡ 3. Navigation Philosophy & 3-Click Rule

Navigation consists of 3 simple layers:

- **Layer 1 (Primary Destinations)**: The 7 Canonical Life Destinations (`HOME`, `TODAY`, `BUILD`, `LEARN`, `GROW`, `THINK`, `REVIEW`).
- **Layer 2 (Context Workspaces)**: Projects, Books, Finances, Notes, Habits.
- **Layer 3 (Object Inspectors)**: Individual project drawer, book highlights, decision audit record.

*Rule: Users should never navigate deeper than 3 levels.*

## ➕ 4. Global Quick Capture & Command Palette
- **Command Palette (`Ctrl + K`)**: Global Spotlight search returning instant results.
- **Floating Quick Capture (`+`)**: Persistent floating action button on all 47 routes for zero-friction capture of Tasks, Notes, Expenses, and Habit logs.
